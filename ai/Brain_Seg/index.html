Few Shot Brain Tissue Segmentation Deep Learning

Why did your best model outperform the baseline?
  • Class-aware loss (α=0.5 Dice+CE weighting) addressed the 66:1 imbalance ratio between largest and smallest classes, improving CSF segmentation most (+0.043 Dice on validation)
  • Denser patch sampling (150 vs 100 patches/subject) provided better coverage of rare structures without overfitting, acting as implicit augmentation.
  • Smaller in-plane patches (64×64 vs 96×96) reduced memory pressure, allowing batch size 8 instead of 2-5, which stabilized gradient estimates.
